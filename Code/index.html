Welcome to Archiviox – where books meet innovation.
Archiviox is a modern Library Management System built to simplify the way libraries operate and how students access knowledge. With a focus on efficiency and ease of use, our platform bridges the gap between traditional libraries and the digital age.
At Archiviox, we believe that learning should be accessible, organized, and inspiring. That’s why we designed a system that empowers both:
  • Students → to search, borrow, and pre-book their favorite titles with ease.
  • Administrators → to manage books, track borrowing history, and maintain records effortlessly.
From smart book tracking to secure logins and intuitive dashboards, Archiviox redefines the library experience for everyone.
Our mission:
To make knowledge seamless, structured, and within reach anytime, anywhere.
